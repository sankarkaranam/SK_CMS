Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how sankarkaranam.com uses cookies and similar tracking technologies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

2. Cookies We Use

This website uses minimal, privacy-respecting cookies only:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies used for admin authentication. These expire when you close your browser or log out. They are never used for third-party tracking.
  • Preference Cookies: Where applicable, we may store light preference settings (such as theme) using browser localStorage, not cookies. No personal data is stored.

3. Analytics

We use privacy-first, server-side analytics to count page views in aggregate. No cookies are set for analytics purposes. No cross-site tracking, no IP-level fingerprinting, no third-party analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics) are loaded unless explicitly stated.

4. Third-Party Services

Some pages may embed content from third-party services (such as LinkedIn share buttons or Google Fonts). These third parties may set their own cookies in accordance with their privacy policies. We recommend reviewing those policies independently.

5. Managing Cookies

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies. Most browsers allow you to manage this in Settings → Privacy & Security. Note that blocking session cookies will prevent admin login functionality.
