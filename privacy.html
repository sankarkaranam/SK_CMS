Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how personal data is handled across sankarkaranam.com and associated subpages.

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and only collect information when explicitly provided by you:

  • Newsletter Subscriptions: When you subscribe to The 1% Daily Compounder, we collect your email address and optional name.
  • Contact Inquiries: When you submit a message via the Contact hub, we collect your name, email, organization, inquiry reason, and message content.
  • Anonymous Telemetry: We collect aggregated, anonymous traffic counters (page visits, general referrers) to optimize performance and content topics. No tracking cookies or cross-site fingerprinting are utilized.

2. How We Use Your Data

Data provided is strictly used to:

  • Deliver requested newsletter editions and insights directly to your inbox.
  • Respond to your partnership, investment, or speaking proposals.
  • Improve platform security and editorial focus based on aggregated interest.

3. Data Protection & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your contact information. Any third-party delivery services (e.g., transactional email APIs) adhere to strict data confidentiality protocols.

4. Your Rights

You may unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the direct link at the bottom of every dispatch, or request full deletion of your contact records by emailing sankarkaranam7@gmail.com.
