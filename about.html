Founder Story

Sankar Karanam

Engineer → Serial Entrepreneur → Founder of 4+ Ventures

I started as an engineer, endlessly curious about how systems work — not just software systems, but business systems, people systems, and compounding systems. That curiosity led me to build my first company before I fully understood what that meant, and I've been building ever since.

Today I operate at the intersection of AI product development, creative media, HR technology, and digital infrastructure. My ventures — OruMind, Creators Club, Worke, and AdPresence — each solve real problems I observed firsthand while building and scaling businesses.

I also wrote a book on cybersecurity, navigated a pandemic shutdown with a food startup, and emerged with a clearer philosophy about what it means to build things that endure.

How I Think

My Core Philosophy

The principles behind every venture, every piece of content, and every decision I make.

01
Compound Small Wins Daily

The 1% philosophy: consistent small improvements compound into extraordinary outcomes. A 1% daily improvement yields a 37× multiplier in a single year. Consistency beats intensity every time.

02
Build Systems, Not Tasks

True scale doesn't come from doing more work. It comes from designing systems, processes, and teams that execute your vision without requiring your direct involvement in every decision.

03
Transparency as a Competitive Advantage

Sharing your raw progress, failures, and breakthroughs openly builds more trust and credibility than any polished marketing campaign. Authenticity is the highest-leverage brand asset.

04
Solve Real Problems First

Every venture I build — OruMind, Worke, AdPresence, Creators Club — began with a genuine problem I observed and couldn't find a satisfying solution for. The best businesses are built on empathy, not opportunity.
