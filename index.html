About

Engineer. Builder. Entrepreneur.

I started as an engineer, curious about how systems work: software systems, business systems, people systems, and the systems that compound over time.

That curiosity led me to build my first company before I fully understood what that meant, and I've been building ever since. Over the last decade I've founded and operated ventures in AI product development, creative media, HR technology and enterprise digital signage.

Along the way I wrote a book on ethical hacking, ran a cybersecurity consultancy, took a food startup through the pandemic shutdown, and came out with a clear way of working: build slow, scale systems, compound daily.
